# Hiring Calendar knowledge-base expansion — 2026-11-13

## Scope and evidence

Work order B expands the authored calendar through the existing idempotent importer and daily refresh. Application instructions and event timing are carried in source JSON, metadata and the public calendar response. Implementation and validation use the local worktree and designated localhost database.

Direct research requests checked each origin’s robots policy first, used an identifying user agent and were paced at least one second apart per host, including redirects. Unavailable or disallowing policies stopped direct content retrieval. Indexed official organiser and government pages supplied additional evidence where direct retrieval was unavailable. Research working files are under ignored `_local/`.

A published date applies only to the named cycle. Historical projections use expected status and month precision. An unknown opening remains unknown even when a deadline is published. Registration, references, corrections, selection rounds and placement dates have distinct meanings. Each source row carries its checked URL and date.

The existing 64 rows receive application instructions and event-window text. Instructions retain their existing source-specific selection stages and direct applicants to the organiser’s current eligibility and document requirements. Detailed document instructions are included where verified. Existing date decisions are preserved.

## Coverage

- Before expansion, 32 of 82 registry programmes had calendar presence and 50 were uncovered.
- Of those 50 gaps, 46 now have dated or explicitly justified undated entries. Four have inactive watchlist records with the reasons below.
- All 78 active entries in the original registry have presence. The importer covers 151 active calendar programmes in total.
- 87 rows added, giving 151 source rows. There are 101 dated source rows and 50 justified undated rows.
- 23 government recruitment/exam entries added. Twenty-one have dated registration boundaries. The two TES intake entries stay undated because current officer notices were not retrievable.
- Uncovered active programmes fall from 50 to 0. Undated classification describes timing uncertainty or variable recruitment and carries a per-row reason. Applicants must check the current call to establish whether applications are open.

## Date and eligibility findings

- UPSC’s published 2027 calendar supports exact notification, last-application and exam dates for CSE, ESE, CDS I/II, NDA I/II and CAPF. The calendar reserves the right to revise dates.
- AFCAT 01/2027 has a current official application notice and a 31 January 2027 exam. GATE 2027 has a revised GOAPS opening, several fee-window extensions and a final October special window. Their source notes preserve the time-of-day cutoffs, while the existing calendar parser stores day precision.
- SSC’s 2026–27 tentative calendar describes 2026 CGL, CHSL and JE exams. The stored 2027 months are historical projections. IBPS’s calendar is also tentative and its year heading is not a promise of 2027 examination dates.
- Army and Air Force recruiting-year/intake labels differ from their registration years. The next registration-year projections explicitly preserve that distinction.
- NIC’s winter registration opening and December–January placement are published. The summer registration month was not verified. NIC remains distinct from MeitY.
- MIT MSRP requires study at a US institution. Its registry eligibility was corrected. The NUS Global Relations source describes outward partner research exchanges, so the registry description now reflects the actual source.
- Amgen’s dated row represents the Asia-host route, with separate host applications. Commonwealth’s dated row represents the Masters award. Fulbright-Nehru remains award-dependent because its categories have different deadlines.
- Unknown registration boundaries remain visible as undated entries for TES, NIC summer and other call-dependent programmes. The per-row reasons below are reviewable limitations on calendar precision.
